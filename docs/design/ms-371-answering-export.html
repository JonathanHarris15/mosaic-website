What is new here
Proposed primitive · Choice

The library has m-check but no single-select answer row. These 52px rows are drawn here as one — m-choice, with m-choice--selected — because ten more question types will all need it.

Proposed primitive · Promise

The anonymous block is a m-scripture-shaped gold-edged panel rather than a m-notice: a notice reads as a system message, and this is the church speaking.

The cap the layout wants

Question text at 20px serif in a 390px phone runs three lines at 92 characters (1f, question 1). 100 characters is where it stops reading as a question; 140 is the hard cap. Form titles: 70 — the 38-character title here already takes two lines at 29px.
