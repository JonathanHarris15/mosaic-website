1 · One-off Event Details: Save button → live page (MS-720)

Today: a Details card with a Save button. Proposed: the values are the controls, a chip in the header, a 1.5 s autosave, and blur or Enter flushes. A change by someone else lands in any field you have not touched. Your own unsaved field is left alone. An end date before the start does not save, and the field says why.
