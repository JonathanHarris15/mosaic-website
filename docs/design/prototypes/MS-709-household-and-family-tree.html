The model

A Household is the unit: a husband and wife, or a single parent, and their unmarried children. No children is fine. A child who marries leaves and starts their own. A Family is the tree those Households make when you follow each child into the Household they started — grandparents, parents, children, grandchildren.

Only the Household is stored. It is the record the directory already keeps (families: husband, wife, children), so no data moves. The Family tree is worked out from Households and is edited by editing them: “add parents” means “say which Household this person grew up in”; “married” means “start their Household”.

What I need decided

  1. Tree layout: A (generation rows), B (outline) or C (drawn pedigree)?
  2. The kiosk’s “Household”. Today the foyer groups anyone who arrives together — a couple and a grandmother, say — and calls it a Household. Under your definition that is not a Household. Rename the foyer one (e.g. “Check-in group”), derive it from the new Household, or keep both?
  3. Where Family lives in the data: keep the stored record as families and call it Household in the UI (no migration), or rename the collection too?
  4. Edge cases you haven’t ruled on: remarriage and step-children, and a child of separated parents who belongs in two Households. Today a person is a child in at most one record, so the error state above is what they would see.
  5. Who edits: editors can edit today; should elders and Pastoral Assistants edit from the Shepherding Profile as shown, or only read?
