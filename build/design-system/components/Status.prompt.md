**Status** — A short fact about a person or a row: Member, Away, Draft. Sentence case, a tint, not a tracked stamp.

```html
<span class="m-status m-status--info">Member</span>
<span class="m-status">Inactive</span>
```

**variant:** `neutral` · `info` · `good` · `warn`

Base class `.m-status`, modifiers `.m-status--<variant>`.

- One or two words. It sits beside a name, never in place of one.
- Do not uppercase it and do not track it out. The word is the fact.

Built from the Mosaic tokens only — no raw colours, no second icon set.
Icons are Material Symbols Outlined.
