Order of Service assistant

There is no website here. This address exists so an AI assistant, such as Claude, can connect to Mosaic and help plan a Sunday's Order of Service.

To connect one, add https://mosaic-hymn-mcp.web.app/mcp as a connector in your assistant. You will be asked to sign in with your usual Mosaic account, and you will need editor access.

Looking for the church website? It is at mosaic-hymn-database.web.app.
